Quick answers

  • Why is the board empty at night? The metro doesn't run from about 01:00 to 05:00. The board says there are no departures in the next hour and fills again when the first trains are listed.
  • Why does it show a Stockholm station when I'm far away? Nästa always shows the nearest Stockholm metro station, however far away, and says how far. The departures are the real ones for that station.
  • How do I change station? Tap the T at the top right to choose one of the five nearest stations.
  • How do I show only one line? Tap a line's dot.
  • Why doesn't the widget update every minute? iOS decides how often a widget refreshes. The minutes count down between refreshes. Opening Nästa or tapping the widget's refresh button updates it.
  • Location is off. Open iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Nästa and choose While Using the App.
  • How do I reset Nästa? Open Settings in the app and tap Reset Nästa. It clears your choices and the welcome screen comes back.
  • Does it show buses, trams or commuter trains? No. Nästa covers the Stockholm Metro only.
