Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Nästa uses your location on your iPhone to find the nearest Stockholm metro station. It has no account, sends nothing about you to the developer, and collects no data.

At a glance

  • Your location is used on your iPhone to pick the nearest station. It is never stored and never sent anywhere.
  • The only request goes to SL's open departure service, and it contains a station's id, not where you are.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party code.

Your location

  • Only while you use it. Nästa asks for location While Using the App, never Always. Once the widget uses location, iOS words the choice While Using the App or Widgets.
  • When it is used. The app uses your location while its board is on screen. The widget uses it only when iOS refreshes the widget.
  • What happens to it. The coordinate is used on the device to pick the nearest station. It is not stored and not sent anywhere.
  • You can change the permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Nästa.

The one network request

To show departures, Nästa asks SL's open Transport API (transport.integration.sl.se), published through Trafiklab, for the departures of one station. The request contains that station's id. Like any web server, SL's server sees the time of the request, your device's IP address and the usual request details, such as the app name and iOS version. How SL handles requests is governed by SL's own policies. Nästa sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

Stored on your iPhone

  • Your settings: sign style, sign language, departures per sign, the line you chose for each station, and whether the welcome screen has been seen.
  • The widget's last board: one station's departures. It never contains a location.
  • The app and the widget share these on the device through an App Group. They never leave it.
  • Settings > Reset Nästa removes them, and so does deleting the app.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising and no third-party code.
  • Nothing about you is sent to the developer. In the App Store's privacy answers this is "Data Not Collected".

Links

Settings has a link to the developer's Instagram profile. It opens in Safari only if you tap it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.
